ABOUT US & THE CHALLENGE

The Real Challenge Facing Indian CAs

Chartered Accountants in India manage hundreds of active clients. When the Income Tax Department (ITD) or GST Network issues formal scrutiny or demand notices, compliance becomes chaotic. CAs spend countless hours logging into individual portals, chasing clients over WhatsApp/Email for documents, and scrambling to meet statutory deadlines.

TECHNULA Solution — NoticeRadar

NoticeRadar bridges this gap completely. It automatically logs into government portals to fetch new notices, utilizes built-in legal AI to analyze complex 10-page notices in seconds, provides a simplified Client Upload Portal, and auto-drafts legally compliant reply letters — all from a single dashboard.
